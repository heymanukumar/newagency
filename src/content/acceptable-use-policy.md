# Acceptable Use Policy

**Effective Date:** 1 September 2026
**Last Updated:** 1 September 2026

## 1. Introduction

This Acceptable Use Policy (“Policy”) defines permitted and prohibited use of websites, applications, software, platforms, products, accounts, APIs, and services owned or operated by Amazonis IT Services Pvt. Ltd. (“Amazonis,” “Company,” “we,” “our,” or “us”).

This Policy applies to all users, customers, clients, vendors, partners, account holders, and authorised representatives.

## 2. Parent Company and Products

Amazonis IT Services Pvt. Ltd. is the parent legal entity and owner/operator of its products and brands, including:

* **Venuefy™** — an online venue discovery and booking marketplace; and
* **Slour** — a subscription-based social media management and artificial-intelligence software platform.

Product-specific rules may supplement this Policy.

## 3. Lawful Use

You must use Amazonis services only:

* For lawful purposes;
* According to applicable agreements and policies;
* Within authorised account permissions;
* Without harming Amazonis, its users, clients, partners, or third parties;
* In compliance with applicable intellectual-property, privacy, consumer, cybersecurity, and platform rules.

## 4. Prohibited Content and Conduct

You must not use our services to create, upload, store, distribute, promote, or facilitate:

* Illegal activity;
* Fraud or impersonation;
* Misleading or deceptive content;
* Harassment, threats, or abuse;
* Defamatory or unlawfully discriminatory material;
* Obscene or exploitative content;
* Content violating privacy or publicity rights;
* Intellectual-property infringement;
* Malware or malicious code;
* Phishing or credential theft;
* Spam or unsolicited mass communication;
* Unauthorised surveillance;
* Circumvention of laws or platform safeguards.

## 5. Security Restrictions

You must not:

* Attempt unauthorised access;
* Probe or test systems without permission;
* Bypass authentication or access controls;
* Interfere with website or platform availability;
* Upload viruses, ransomware, or harmful code;
* Exploit vulnerabilities;
* Share credentials with unauthorised persons;
* Use another person’s account without authority;
* Conceal the source of malicious activity;
* Conduct denial-of-service attacks;
* Access data not intended for you.

Security vulnerabilities should be reported responsibly to [info@amazonis.in](mailto:info@amazonis.in).

## 6. Automated Access and Resource Abuse

Unless expressly authorised, you must not:

* Scrape or systematically extract data;
* Use bots to create accounts or transactions;
* Overload infrastructure;
* Circumvent usage or rate limits;
* Resell access without permission;
* Copy or mirror substantial portions of a service;
* Reverse engineer software except where legally permitted;
* Manipulate credits, referrals, payments, reviews, or analytics.

## 7. Intellectual Property

You must have lawful rights to any content, media, data, software, trademark, music, video, or other material submitted through our services.

You must not use our services to infringe:

* Copyright;
* Trademark;
* Patent;
* Trade-secret rights;
* Database rights;
* Publicity or personality rights;
* Other proprietary rights.

Amazonis may restrict or remove material subject to a credible infringement complaint.

## 8. Privacy and Personal Information

You must not collect, upload, disclose, or process another person’s information without lawful authority.

You must use appropriate notices, consent, security, and access restrictions where required.

Sensitive credentials, OTPs, passwords, UPI PINs, CVVs, and complete payment credentials must not be submitted through ordinary support channels.

## 9. Slour-Specific Use

Slour users must:

* Connect only accounts they are authorised to manage;
* Follow the rules of connected social-media platforms;
* Review content before publication;
* Maintain necessary licences and permissions;
* Protect client and account credentials;
* Respect messaging and anti-spam rules;
* Monitor automated or scheduled content.

Slour must not be used to:

* Create fake engagement;
* Operate unauthorised accounts;
* Manipulate platform metrics;
* Spread malicious misinformation;
* Conduct unlawful mass messaging;
* Circumvent platform restrictions;
* Publish infringing or prohibited content;
* Misrepresent AI-generated content where disclosure is legally required.

## 10. Venuefy™-Specific Use

Venuefy™ customers and venue partners must not:

* Submit false booking information;
* Create fraudulent enquiries;
* Misrepresent venue ownership;
* Publish false prices, availability, amenities, or capacity;
* Upload unauthorised venue media;
* Circumvent applicable booking or commission terms;
* Harass customers, venue representatives, or staff;
* Manipulate ratings or reviews;
* Collect or misuse customer information;
* Use the platform for unlawful events or transactions.

Venue partners are responsible for maintaining accurate listing and availability information.

## 11. Payment and Subscription Abuse

You must not:

* Use unauthorised payment methods;
* Initiate knowingly false chargebacks;
* Manipulate subscriptions or credits;
* Exploit payment errors;
* Use fraudulent identity or billing information;
* Attempt duplicate refunds;
* Avoid payment for used services;
* Resell subscriptions without permission.

Amazonis may suspend access while investigating suspected payment fraud.

## 12. Customer and Vendor Communications

Users must communicate respectfully and professionally.

Amazonis may restrict users engaging in:

* Threats or harassment;
* Discriminatory abuse;
* Repeated spam;
* Fraudulent representation;
* Deliberate interference with support;
* Attempts to obtain confidential information.

## 13. Monitoring and Investigation

Amazonis may monitor service usage to the extent reasonably necessary to:

* Maintain security;
* Prevent fraud;
* Enforce this Policy;
* Meet legal obligations;
* Investigate reported misuse;
* Protect users and infrastructure.

Monitoring will be conducted according to applicable privacy requirements.

## 14. Enforcement

If Amazonis reasonably believes this Policy has been violated, it may:

* Issue a warning;
* Remove or restrict content;
* Limit features;
* Suspend an account;
* Terminate access;
* Preserve relevant records;
* Notify an affected third party;
* Report unlawful activity to an appropriate authority;
* Take other lawful action.

Where appropriate, Amazonis may provide an opportunity to correct the violation.

Immediate action may be taken where necessary to prevent fraud, harm, illegality, or a security incident.

## 15. Reporting Violations

Report suspected violations to:

**Email:** [info@amazonis.in](mailto:info@amazonis.in)
**Subject:** Acceptable Use or Security Report

Include sufficient information and evidence to allow review. Do not publicly disclose security vulnerabilities before Amazonis has a reasonable opportunity to investigate.

## 16. Changes to This Policy

Amazonis may update this Policy to reflect changes in its services, products, risks, technology, or legal requirements.

Continued use after an applicable update is subject to the updated Policy.
