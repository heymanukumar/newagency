# Cancellation Policy

**Effective Date:** 1 September 2026
**Last Updated:** 1 September 2026

## 1. Introduction

This Cancellation Policy explains how customers may cancel services, projects, subscriptions, orders, or other arrangements purchased from Amazonis IT Services Pvt. Ltd. (“Amazonis,” “Company,” “we,” “our,” or “us”).

This Policy should be read with our Terms and Conditions, Refund Policy, Service Delivery/Fulfilment Policy, applicable quotation or agreement, and product-specific policies.

Cancellation of a service does not automatically make previous payments refundable. Refund eligibility will be determined separately under the applicable Refund Policy and agreement.

## 2. Parent Company and Products

Amazonis IT Services Pvt. Ltd. is the parent legal entity and owner/operator of its products and brands, including:

* **Venuefy™** — an online venue discovery and booking marketplace; and
* **Slour** — a subscription-based social media management and artificial-intelligence software platform.

Venuefy™ and Slour are not separate legal entities.

Product-specific cancellation conditions may apply. Where they conflict with this general Policy, the product-specific conditions will apply to the relevant transaction.

## 3. Cancellation Before Work Begins

A customer may request cancellation before Amazonis begins work or allocates material resources.

Any refund will be assessed after deducting, where applicable:

* Work already completed;
* Consultation, discovery, planning, or onboarding costs;
* Non-recoverable payment-processing charges;
* Third-party purchases or commitments;
* Amounts identified as non-refundable in the accepted agreement.

Cancellation is effective only after written confirmation from Amazonis.

## 4. Cancellation After Work Begins

If cancellation is requested after work begins:

* Completed work remains payable;
* Approved milestones are non-refundable;
* Work performed up to the cancellation date will be calculated;
* Third-party expenses and committed resources remain payable;
* Undelivered future work will stop after cancellation is confirmed;
* Access to unpaid or incomplete deliverables may be withheld.

Amazonis may provide completed work in its then-current form after all outstanding amounts are paid, subject to the applicable agreement.

## 5. Milestone-Based Projects

For milestone-based projects:

* Cancellation applies only to future work not already performed;
* Completed and approved milestones cannot be cancelled retrospectively;
* Work submitted for review will be considered when calculating the completed portion;
* Delayed feedback or approval from the customer does not cancel payment obligations;
* Future milestones may be cancelled according to the written agreement.

## 6. Recurring Services and Subscriptions

Recurring services may be cancelled through the relevant account dashboard or by submitting a written request.

Unless product-specific terms state otherwise:

* Cancellation stops future renewals;
* Access may continue until the end of the paid billing period;
* Cancellation does not automatically refund the current billing period;
* Unused time, features, or credits are not automatically refundable;
* Requests should be submitted before the next renewal date;
* A payment already initiated may not be reversible.

Slour subscriptions will be governed by the Slour Subscription and Cancellation Policy.

Deleting an application or abandoning an account does not cancel an active subscription or payment mandate.

## 7. Venuefy™ Bookings

Venuefy™ booking cancellations will be governed by:

* Venuefy™ Booking Terms;
* Venuefy™ Cancellation and Refund Policies;
* The selected venue’s disclosed conditions;
* The confirmed booking details;
* The time remaining before the event;
* Services and resources already reserved.

Submitting a Venuefy™ cancellation request does not itself cancel the booking. Cancellation is effective only after confirmation through an authorised channel.

## 8. Third-Party Services

The following may not be cancellable or refundable after purchase or activation:

* Domains;
* Hosting and cloud services;
* SSL certificates;
* Software licences;
* Themes, plugins, and templates;
* API services;
* App-store charges;
* Advertising expenditure;
* Stock assets;
* Communication credits;
* Other third-party services.

Where a third party permits cancellation, its terms and deductions will apply.

## 9. Customer-Requested Pause

A request to pause work is not automatically treated as cancellation.

A paused project may be subject to:

* Revised timelines;
* Resource reallocation;
* Restart or reactivation charges where disclosed;
* Updated pricing if the pause is prolonged;
* Cancellation after an extended period of inactivity.

The applicable agreement may specify the permitted pause period.

## 10. Cancellation by Amazonis

Amazonis may suspend or cancel a service where:

* Payment is overdue, reversed, or disputed;
* The customer materially breaches applicable terms;
* Fraud, abuse, or unlawful activity is suspected;
* Required information or cooperation is repeatedly withheld;
* Continuing the service creates a security or legal risk;
* A third-party dependency becomes unavailable;
* Delivery becomes unlawful or reasonably impossible;
* The customer engages in abusive or threatening conduct.

Where reasonably appropriate, Amazonis will provide notice and an opportunity to correct the issue.

If Amazonis permanently cancels a prepaid service without customer fault, Amazonis may provide an appropriate refund or credit for the substantially undelivered portion, after lawful deductions.

## 11. How to Request Cancellation

Email cancellation requests to **[info@amazonis.in](mailto:info@amazonis.in)** with the subject:

**Cancellation Request – [Order/Invoice/Project Number]**

Include:

* Customer’s full name;
* Registered email and mobile number;
* Service, product, project, or booking name;
* Order, invoice, subscription, or transaction number;
* Reason for cancellation;
* Requested cancellation date;
* Relevant supporting information.

Do not send passwords, OTPs, PINs, CVVs, or complete card information.

## 12. Confirmation

A cancellation request is not considered accepted until Amazonis sends written confirmation.

Amazonis may request identity or account verification before processing the request.

The customer remains responsible for charges incurred before the effective cancellation date.

## 13. Refunds Following Cancellation

Any refund following cancellation will be assessed under the applicable Refund Policy.

Approved refunds will generally be initiated within 7 to 10 business days after written approval. Banking or payment-gateway processing may require additional time.

## 14. Chargebacks

Cancelling a service does not authorise an invalid chargeback.

Customers should contact Amazonis before initiating a payment dispute so that the matter can be reviewed.

Nothing prevents a customer from exercising a legitimate right available under applicable law.

## 15. Changes to This Policy

Amazonis may update this Policy to reflect changes in its products, services, business practices, or legal requirements.

The version applicable at the time of purchase will generally govern the relevant transaction unless otherwise required by law.
