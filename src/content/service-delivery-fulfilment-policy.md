# Service Delivery and Fulfilment Policy

**Effective Date:** 1 September 2026
**Last Updated:** 1 September 2026

## 1. Introduction

This Service Delivery and Fulfilment Policy explains how Amazonis IT Services Pvt. Ltd. (“Amazonis,” “Company,” “we,” “our,” or “us”) delivers its digital, professional, technology, subscription, and marketplace-related services.

Amazonis primarily provides digital services. Unless expressly stated otherwise, no physical product is manufactured or shipped.

## 2. Parent Company and Products

Amazonis IT Services Pvt. Ltd. is the parent legal entity and owner/operator of its products and brands, including:

* **Venuefy™** — an online venue discovery and booking marketplace; and
* **Slour** — a subscription-based social media management and artificial-intelligence software platform.

Product-specific fulfilment conditions may supplement this Policy.

## 3. Types of Services

Amazonis may provide:

* Website design and development;
* Web and mobile application development;
* Custom software development;
* E-commerce development;
* Hosting and maintenance;
* Digital marketing;
* Social media management;
* Artificial-intelligence solutions;
* Technology consulting;
* SaaS subscriptions;
* Marketplace and booking-platform services;
* Other digital and IT-related services.

## 4. Delivery Methods

Depending on the service, delivery may occur through:

* Email;
* Secure download link;
* Cloud storage;
* Source-code repository;
* Staging or production server;
* Customer dashboard;
* User account activation;
* Online meeting or presentation;
* Deployment to the customer’s hosting environment;
* Third-party platform;
* Written confirmation of booking or service activation.

The applicable proposal, quotation, agreement, or product page will identify the relevant delivery method.

## 5. No Physical Shipping

Amazonis primarily sells services, software, subscriptions, and digital solutions.

Accordingly:

* No physical shipping is ordinarily required;
* No courier tracking number will normally be issued;
* Delivery occurs electronically or through online service activation;
* A “shipping address” requested by a payment provider may be used only for billing, verification, or compliance.

If a transaction specifically includes a physical item, separate delivery terms will be communicated before purchase.

## 6. Project Commencement

Project work will generally begin after:

* The proposal or quotation is approved;
* The required agreement is accepted;
* The applicable advance payment is received;
* The customer provides required content, information, credentials, and access;
* Project requirements are reasonably clear.

An estimated start date is not guaranteed until these requirements are completed.

## 7. Delivery Timelines

Delivery timelines depend on:

* Project scope and complexity;
* Agreed milestones;
* Timely customer payment;
* Availability of content and access;
* Customer feedback and approvals;
* Change requests;
* Third-party services and integrations;
* Technical requirements;
* Events beyond reasonable control.

The applicable written proposal or agreement will contain the primary delivery estimate.

A timeline may be revised where the customer delays content, approval, payment, or access.

## 8. Milestone Delivery

Projects may be divided into milestones such as:

* Discovery and planning;
* Wireframes or design;
* Development;
* Testing;
* Customer review;
* Deployment;
* Handover;
* Maintenance.

A milestone may be delivered for review through email, a project-management tool, staging link, repository, or online demonstration.

Approval of a milestone may allow the next phase to begin.

## 9. Customer Review and Acceptance

The customer must review deliverables and report material issues within the review period stated in the applicable agreement.

If no review period is specified, the customer should provide feedback within seven calendar days of delivery.

The deliverable may be treated as accepted where:

* The customer expressly approves it;
* The customer begins commercial use;
* The customer publishes or deploys it;
* The customer fails to provide feedback after reasonable reminders.

Acceptance does not remove obligations relating to defects covered by an agreed support or warranty period.

## 10. Revisions and Scope Changes

Included revisions are limited to the scope stated in the applicable quotation or agreement.

The following may be treated as additional work:

* New pages or features;
* New integrations;
* Major design changes after approval;
* Change of technology;
* Rebuilding approved work;
* Requirements not included in the original scope.

Additional work may require revised pricing and delivery timelines.

## 11. Digital Subscriptions and Slour

Slour access may be activated after successful payment or mandate authorisation.

Depending on the plan:

* Features may activate immediately or within a reasonable technical-processing period;
* Usage credits may be applied to the account;
* Access remains subject to plan limits;
* Recurring access depends on successful renewal payment;
* Failed or reversed payments may result in restricted access;
* Upgrades and downgrades may apply according to product-specific terms.

A confirmation may be provided through the dashboard, email, or another registered communication channel.

## 12. Venuefy™ Fulfilment

Venuefy™ may facilitate:

* Venue discovery;
* Enquiries;
* Availability requests;
* Booking communication;
* Online booking payments;
* Booking confirmation;
* Venue-owner settlements.

A Venuefy™ enquiry is not a confirmed booking.

A booking is considered confirmed only after the required steps shown during checkout or confirmation are completed.

The actual venue and event-related services are fulfilled by the selected venue owner or service provider according to the confirmed booking terms.

## 13. Third-Party Dependencies

Delivery may depend on third parties such as:

* Hosting providers;
* Domain registrars;
* Payment gateways;
* Social-media platforms;
* Cloud providers;
* API providers;
* Application stores;
* Email and communication platforms.

Amazonis is not responsible for delays or failures caused solely by third-party systems outside its reasonable control. We will make reasonable efforts to provide an alternative or revised timeline where practical.

## 14. Customer Responsibilities

The customer must provide:

* Accurate requirements;
* Required content and media;
* Lawful access and credentials;
* Timely approvals;
* A designated contact person;
* Payments according to agreed milestones;
* Required third-party accounts;
* Reasonable cooperation during testing and delivery.

Customer-caused delays may automatically extend the delivery timeline.

## 15. Delivery Failure or Material Defect

If a customer believes a deliverable was not received or contains a material defect, the customer should contact Amazonis with:

* Order or invoice number;
* Project or service name;
* Description of the issue;
* Relevant screenshots or evidence;
* Date of expected or attempted delivery.

Amazonis may investigate and, where appropriate:

* Re-send the deliverable;
* Correct the verified defect;
* Restore access;
* Provide a revised timeline;
* Offer another resolution under the applicable agreement.

## 16. Support and Maintenance

Post-delivery support is limited to the period and scope stated in the relevant agreement, quotation, or subscription plan.

Support does not automatically include:

* New features;
* Redesign;
* Content creation;
* Third-party changes;
* Damage caused by unauthorised modifications;
* Issues outside the agreed technology;
* Work after the support period.

Additional maintenance may be purchased separately.

## 17. Force Majeure

Delivery may be delayed by events beyond reasonable control, including natural disasters, government actions, internet failures, cloud outages, cyber incidents, banking failures, public emergencies, or third-party disruptions.

Amazonis will make reasonable efforts to communicate a revised timeline.
