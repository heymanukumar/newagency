# Refund Policy

**Effective Date:** 1 September 2026
**Last Updated:** 1 September 2026

## 1. Introduction

This Refund Policy explains the circumstances in which a customer may request and receive a refund for services, digital products, subscriptions, projects, or other solutions purchased from Amazonis IT Services Pvt. Ltd. (“Amazonis,” “Company,” “we,” “our,” or “us”).

This Refund Policy should be read together with our:

* Terms and Conditions;
* Cancellation Policy;
* Service Delivery/Fulfilment Policy;
* Applicable quotation, proposal, invoice, order form, or agreement;
* Product-specific terms and policies.

By making a payment to Amazonis, you acknowledge that you have reviewed and accepted this Refund Policy.

## 2. Parent Company, Products and Brands

Amazonis IT Services Pvt. Ltd. is the parent legal entity and owner/operator of its digital products and brands, including:

* **Venuefy™** — an online venue discovery and booking marketplace; and
* **Slour** — a subscription-based social media management and artificial-intelligence software platform.

Venuefy™ and Slour are products owned and operated by Amazonis IT Services Pvt. Ltd. They are not separate legal entities.

Product-specific refund terms may apply to payments made through Venuefy™, Slour, or another Amazonis product.

If a product-specific Refund Policy conflicts with this general Refund Policy, the product-specific policy will apply to the relevant payment, booking, subscription, or transaction.

## 3. General Refund Principle

Refund eligibility will depend on:

* The type of service or product purchased;
* The stage of work completed;
* Whether service delivery has started;
* Whether digital access has been activated;
* Resources already allocated;
* Third-party costs already incurred;
* Deliverables already submitted or approved;
* The reason for the refund request;
* Applicable quotation, agreement, or product-specific terms;
* Applicable law.

Submitting a refund request does not automatically guarantee that a refund will be approved.

Amazonis will review each valid request fairly based on the relevant transaction and supporting information.

## 4. Refunds Before Work Begins

A customer may request cancellation and refund before Amazonis begins work or allocates material resources to the project.

Where approved, the refundable amount may exclude:

* Non-recoverable payment-gateway charges;
* Bank or transaction charges;
* Third-party costs already paid;
* Domain, hosting, licence, software, advertising, or API costs already incurred;
* Work already completed during discovery, consultation, planning, research, or onboarding;
* Any amount expressly identified as non-refundable in an accepted proposal or agreement.

The customer should submit the request as soon as possible after payment.

## 5. Refunds After Work Has Started

Once project work or service delivery has started, a full refund will generally not be available.

Where appropriate, Amazonis may calculate a partial refund based on:

* The uncompleted portion of the agreed work;
* Completed milestones;
* Time and resources already used;
* Work already delivered;
* Approved designs, content, code, reports, or other deliverables;
* Non-recoverable third-party expenses;
* Contractual cancellation charges, where applicable.

Fees relating to completed work and committed third-party expenses will not be refundable.

Any partial refund is subject to review and written approval by Amazonis.

## 6. Completed and Delivered Services

A refund will generally not be available where:

* The agreed service has been completed;
* Final files, code, credentials, reports, or deliverables have been delivered;
* The customer has approved the deliverable;
* The service has been accepted or commercially used;
* The customer failed to respond or provide feedback within the agreed review period;
* The issue results from a change in customer preference after approval;
* The customer requests work outside the agreed scope;
* The customer no longer requires the completed service.

Nothing in this section limits a right or remedy that cannot legally be excluded.

## 7. Deposits and Advance Payments

Advance payments may be used to:

* Reserve project capacity;
* Conduct research and planning;
* Begin design or development;
* Assign team members;
* Purchase third-party services;
* Perform onboarding or consultation.

An advance payment is not automatically refundable after work begins or resources are committed.

Refund eligibility for an advance payment will be determined according to:

* Work already performed;
* Costs already incurred;
* The accepted proposal or agreement;
* The reason and timing of cancellation;
* Applicable law.

Where an agreement expressly identifies a reasonable booking, onboarding, or mobilisation amount as non-refundable, that term will apply to the extent permitted by law.

## 8. Milestone-Based Projects

For projects divided into milestones:

* Payments for completed or approved milestones are non-refundable;
* Work submitted for review will be considered when calculating any refund;
* A refund may be considered only for a prepaid and substantially unperformed future milestone;
* Third-party expenses and approved additional work remain payable;
* Failure by the customer to provide information, content, approval, or access does not make completed work refundable.

Milestone acceptance and review periods may be specified in the applicable agreement.

## 9. Digital Products and Subscriptions

Digital products and subscriptions may provide immediate access to software, features, credits, content, infrastructure, or digital resources.

Unless a product-specific policy states otherwise, refunds may not be available after:

* Digital access has been activated;
* Subscription features have been used;
* Credits or usage limits have been consumed;
* Downloadable content has been accessed;
* A billing cycle has started;
* Third-party resources have been provisioned.

Slour subscriptions will be governed by the Slour Subscription, Cancellation, and Refund Policies.

Cancellation of a recurring subscription normally prevents future renewal. It does not automatically create a refund for a current or previously completed billing period.

## 10. Venuefy™ Payments

Venuefy™ booking payments, platform charges, venue-owner amounts, commissions, and refunds will be governed by:

* Venuefy™ Booking Terms;
* Venuefy™ Cancellation Policy;
* Venuefy™ Refund Policy;
* The applicable venue’s booking conditions;
* The customer’s confirmed booking details;
* Applicable law.

The refundable amount may depend on the booking date, event date, venue-owner terms, services already reserved, and the reason for cancellation.

Amazonis will not apply this general Refund Policy to override a more specific Venuefy™ refund condition accepted during booking.

## 11. Third-Party Costs

Amounts paid or committed to third parties may be non-refundable once purchased, activated, reserved, or consumed.

These may include:

* Domain registrations and renewals;
* Hosting and cloud services;
* SSL certificates;
* Software and plugin licences;
* Themes and templates;
* Stock media;
* API and platform charges;
* App-store fees;
* Advertising expenditure;
* SMS, email, WhatsApp, or communication credits;
* Payment-gateway and banking charges;
* Other third-party products or services.

Where a third party provides a refund, Amazonis may pass the recoverable amount to the customer after applicable adjustments.

## 12. Change of Mind

A refund will generally not be provided solely because:

* The customer changed their mind;
* The customer changed their business plan;
* The customer selected the wrong service or plan;
* The customer no longer requires the service;
* The customer expected a feature not included in the agreed scope;
* The customer did not use an activated service;
* The customer found another provider after work started.

Customers should review all service details, inclusions, exclusions, pricing, and billing terms before making payment.

## 13. Delayed Projects

A project delay does not automatically create a right to a refund where the delay results from:

* Late content, access, information, or approval from the customer;
* Change requests or scope expansion;
* Customer-requested pauses;
* Third-party platform or API delays;
* Payment delays;
* Technical dependencies;
* Events beyond Amazonis’ reasonable control.

Where a material delay is directly caused by Amazonis and Amazonis is unable to provide the affected service within a reasonable revised period, the parties may agree to:

* A revised delivery date;
* A service credit;
* An alternative deliverable;
* A partial refund for the undelivered portion;
* Another reasonable solution.

## 14. Duplicate Payments

If a customer is charged more than once for the same transaction, the customer should contact Amazonis with:

* Name and contact details;
* Order or invoice number;
* Transaction reference numbers;
* Payment date and amount;
* Bank or payment screenshot, where required.

After verification, an approved duplicate payment refund will be initiated to the original payment method.

## 15. Failed, Pending, or Reversed Transactions

A failed or pending payment does not necessarily mean that Amazonis received the amount.

Customers should first check the final status with their bank or payment provider.

Where money is debited but the transaction fails:

* The bank or payment provider may automatically reverse the amount;
* The reversal timeline is controlled by the relevant financial institution;
* Amazonis may assist by providing available transaction information;
* A refund can be initiated by Amazonis only if the amount was successfully received and settled or made available for refund.

## 16. Unauthorised or Fraudulent Payments

A suspected unauthorised payment should be reported immediately to:

* The customer’s bank or payment provider; and
* Amazonis at [info@amazonis.in](mailto:info@amazonis.in).

Amazonis may request identity, account, order, or transaction information before taking action.

Accounts, services, or transactions may be temporarily restricted while the matter is investigated.

## 17. Promotional Offers and Credits

Promotional credits, coupons, complimentary access, bonus features, free trials, and similar benefits:

* Have no cash value;
* Cannot normally be transferred;
* Cannot normally be exchanged for money;
* May expire according to the applicable offer terms;
* May be withdrawn in cases of fraud, misuse, or error.

Where a paid transaction used a discount, any approved refund will be based on the amount actually paid, not the undiscounted price.

## 18. Refund Request Procedure

To request a refund, email [info@amazonis.in](mailto:info@amazonis.in) with the subject:

**Refund Request – [Order/Invoice/Transaction Number]**

The request should include:

* Customer’s full name;
* Registered email address and mobile number;
* Service, product, or project name;
* Order, invoice, or transaction number;
* Payment date and amount;
* Reason for requesting the refund;
* Supporting documents, screenshots, or communications;
* Bank or payment details only where specifically requested through a secure method.

Do not send UPI PINs, CVV details, passwords, OTPs, or complete card credentials.

## 19. Refund Review

After receiving a complete refund request, Amazonis may:

* Verify the payment;
* Review the applicable agreement and policy;
* Check the project or service-delivery status;
* Review resources and third-party costs already committed;
* Request additional information;
* Approve, partially approve, or reject the request;
* Propose an alternative resolution where appropriate.

Amazonis will communicate the outcome through the customer’s registered email address or another verified communication channel.

## 20. Refund Processing Time

An approved refund will generally be initiated within **7 to 10 business days** after written approval.

After initiation, the time taken for the amount to appear in the customer’s account depends on:

* The payment method;
* The customer’s bank;
* The card network;
* The UPI provider;
* The payment gateway;
* Banking holidays and processing timelines.

Amazonis cannot control delays occurring after a refund has been submitted to the banking or payment system.

## 21. Refund Method

Approved refunds will ordinarily be processed to the original payment method.

Where a refund to the original payment method is not technically possible, Amazonis may request verified bank-account information and process the refund through another lawful method.

For security reasons, Amazonis may refuse a request to send a refund to an unrelated person or unverified account.

## 22. Refund Amount

The approved refund amount may be adjusted for:

* Completed work;
* Delivered milestones;
* Used subscription periods;
* Consumed credits;
* Non-recoverable third-party expenses;
* Non-refundable payment-processing costs;
* Applicable contractual deductions;
* Taxes or statutory adjustments where required;
* Previous refunds, credits, or chargebacks.

Any deduction will be assessed according to the applicable agreement, transaction, and law.

## 23. Chargebacks and Payment Disputes

Customers are encouraged to contact Amazonis before initiating a chargeback so that the issue can be reviewed and resolved.

A false, abusive, or duplicate chargeback may result in:

* Suspension of the relevant service;
* Recovery of unpaid amounts;
* Submission of transaction and service records to the payment provider;
* Other lawful action.

Nothing in this section prevents a customer from exercising a legitimate right available through their bank, payment provider, or applicable law.

## 24. Cancellation

A cancellation request is separate from a refund request.

Cancelling a service, project, booking, or subscription does not automatically mean that past payments are refundable.

Cancellation rights and consequences are explained in the applicable Cancellation Policy, product terms, proposal, or agreement.

## 25. Policy Abuse

Amazonis may reject or investigate refund requests involving:

* Fraud;
* Misrepresentation;
* Repeated misuse;
* Fabricated evidence;
* Unauthorised payment disputes;
* Use of the service followed by an abusive refund request;
* Violation of applicable terms;
* Attempts to obtain duplicate compensation.

## 26. Changes to This Refund Policy

Amazonis may update this Refund Policy to reflect changes in services, products, payment processes, or legal requirements.

The updated policy will be published with a revised “Last Updated” date.

The policy applicable at the time of the relevant purchase will generally govern that transaction, unless a change is required by law or expressly accepted by the customer.
