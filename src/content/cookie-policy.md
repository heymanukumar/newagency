## 2. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on a browser or device when a website is visited.

Cookies and similar technologies may help websites:

* Operate correctly;
* Maintain login sessions;
* Remember preferences;
* Protect accounts and transactions;
* Measure usage and performance;
* Diagnose errors;
* Improve user experience;
* Understand marketing effectiveness.

Similar technologies may include pixels, tags, local storage, software-development kits, and device identifiers.

You may manage cookies through:

* Website cookie controls;
* Browser settings;
* Device settings;
* Third-party opt-out tools where available.

* Sign you out;
* Remove saved preferences;
* Affect forms or checkout;
* Limit website or product features;

## 8. Cookie Duration

Cookies may be:

* **Session cookies**, which expire when the browser is closed; or
* **Persistent cookies**, which remain for a defined period or until deleted.

Retention depends on the purpose of the cookie, legal requirements, and provider settings. Cookies should not be retained longer than reasonably necessary for their stated purpose.

## 9. Do Not Track Signals

Some browsers provide “Do Not Track” signals. Because there is no universally accepted standard for responding to these signals, our website may not respond consistently to every such setting.

You may use available cookie controls to manage optional tracking.
